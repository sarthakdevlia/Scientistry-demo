Questions

Asked often

What is Bicomide?

Bicomide is a niacinamide delivery system that uses Bicosome technology. In our Anti Acne Face Serum (3% Bicomide) it is designed to deliver niacinamide to targeted skin layers for acne and sebum control, gently enough for people who find plain niacinamide irritating.

Can I use Vitamin C and an exfoliating acid together?

Keep them apart: use the Vitamin C Face Serum in the morning under sunscreen, and exfoliating serums such as AHA PHA BHA or Salicylic Acid at night.

Which serum is best for oily, acne-prone skin?

The Anti Acne Face Serum (3% Bicomide) or Niacinamide Face Serum in the morning, and the Salicylic Acid Face Serum (2% BHA) at night.

Is the AHA PHA BHA serum for daily use?

No. It is a 20% AHA + 5% PHA + 2% BHA peeling solution meant for once a week, at night, followed by sunscreen the next morning. Sensitive skin can use the gentler Daily Overnight PHA Serum instead.
